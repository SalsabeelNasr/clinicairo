# CliniCairo — Brand Style & Social Media Guidelines

Derived from the site design, copy (`messages/ar.json`), and UI components.

---

| Element | Guideline |
|--------|-----------|
| **Name** | **CliniCairo** — always capitalized as shown. Never "Clinic Cairo", "CliniCairo", or "ClinicCairo" |
| **Logo** | `CliniCairo` + solid **cyan dot** immediately after the word (like a period). Dot = primary brand cyan |
| **Logo direction** | Always **LTR** (left-to-right), even on Arabic posts |
| **What you do** | Remote weight-loss tele-clinic: Egyptian specialists supervise patients **outside Egypt** (Libya, Sudan, etc.) while they use GLP-1 injections locally |
| **Arabic descriptor** | عيادة إنقاص الوزن عن بُعد |

## 2. Color palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|--------|
| **Primary (Cyan)** | `#06b6d4` | Logo dot, buttons, highlights, icons, links, accent blocks |
| **Primary on dark** | `#ffffff` | Text/icons on cyan backgrounds |
| **Background** | `#ffffff` | Default canvas — clean, clinical, airy |
| **Headline text** | `#0f172a` (slate-900) | Titles, bold statements |
| **Body text** | `#64748b` (muted gray) | Supporting copy, captions |
| **Light cyan** | `#67e8f9` | Gradients, soft glows, decorative accents |
| **Borders/dividers** | `#f1f5f9` | Cards, frames |
| **WhatsApp CTA** | `#25D366` | WhatsApp buttons/icons only |

**Color rules**

- **60% white**, **30% dark text**, **10% cyan accent**
- Cyan works best as: dot, CTA button, icon circles, underline/highlight, thin borders
- Avoid yellow/gold or pink — removed from the brand
- Photo overlays: **20% white wash** (`bg-white/20`) over lifestyle/medical imagery

## 3. Typography

| Language | Primary font | Fallback fonts |
|----------|--------------|----------------|
| **Arabic** | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** | **Cairo**, **Noto Sans Arabic**, system Arabic sans-serif |
| **English (logo only)** | Clean geometric sans (Inter, Helvetica) | **IBM Plex Sans**, system sans-serif |

**Weights**

- Body: 400
- Subheads: 500
- Headlines & CTAs: 600–700

**Hierarchy**

- **Headlines:** Bold/extrabold, tight tracking, `#0f172a`
- **Subheads/leads:** Regular/medium, relaxed line height
- **Captions:** Smaller, muted gray
- **CTAs:** Semibold, inside **pill-shaped (fully rounded)** buttons

**RTL rule:** Arabic posts are **right-to-left**. Keep **CliniCairo●** logo LTR in a corner or header strip.

## 4. Visual style

**Overall feel:** Modern medical clinic — trustworthy, calm, premium, not flashy.

**Do use**

- White space
- Soft rounded corners (8–16px cards; **full pills** on buttons)
- Cyan circular icon badges with white icons
- Real photography: consultations, healthy lifestyle, medical professionalism
- Light shadows tinted cyan (~20% opacity)
- Simple layouts: headline → 2–3 bullets → CTA

**Avoid**

- Busy gradients (except subtle cyan → `#67e8f9` at low opacity)
- Heavy filters, neon colors, aggressive sales graphics
- Exaggerated or non-medical "before/after" imagery
- Dense text blocks

**Layout patterns**

1. **Hero** — bold headline + 3 bullets with cyan check circles + WhatsApp CTA
2. **Pain point** — empathetic question + 3 problem/solution cards
3. **Process carousel** — Today → Week 1 → Weeks 2–4
4. **Trust** — doctor supervision + team + AI assistant (3 columns)
5. **CTA strip** — full-width cyan band, white text, "تواصل الآن"

---

## 5. Logo usage

```
CliniCairo●   ← dot = #06b6d4, ~45% of cap height
```

- Minimum clear space: height of the "C" around the logo
- On photos: white or light area, or subtle white pill behind logo
- **Don't** stretch, recolor the wordmark, drop the dot, or use all-caps
- **Don't** place on busy backgrounds without contrast

## 6. Voice & tone (Arabic-first)

**Personality:** Expert, warm, direct, reassuring — like a good Egyptian specialist explaining things clearly.

**Language register:** Use **accessible Modern Standard Arabic** ("White Arabic") — formal enough to feel medical and trustworthy, but conversational enough for Libya, Sudan, and the Gulf. Avoid heavy Egyptian slang; avoid overly classical/formal phrasing that feels distant.

| Trait | Example |
|-------|---------|
| **Empathetic** | "هل تحس إن الرحلة وحدها صعبة؟" |
| **Expert** | "بإشراف نخبة من الأطباء الاستشاريين المصريين" |
| **Transparent** | "شفافية كاملة في التكاليف" |
| **Action-oriented** | "ابدأ رحلة إنقاص الوزن" / "تواصل واتساب" |
| **Personal** | Often addresses the patient as **أنتِ** (feminine) — stay consistent within a post |

**Messaging pillars**

1. Medical supervision by Egyptian obesity specialists
2. Follow-up from home (Libya, Sudan, outside Egypt)
3. Personalized plans based on labs and health history
4. GLP-1 / injection weight-loss under doctor care
5. Full team: doctors + nutrition + AI follow-up bot
6. No hidden fees, structured process

**Avoid**

- Guaranteed weight-loss claims
- Medical advice without "تحت إشراف طبيب"
- Fear-based or shame-based language
- Positioning AI as a replacement for doctors

**Disclaimer tone:** Treatments are under physician supervision; remote care does not replace emergency local care.

## 7. Social media aspect ratios

| Format | Ratio | Use |
|--------|-------|-----|
| **Instagram/Facebook feed post** | **1:1** or **4:5** | Single images, carousels |
| **Instagram/Facebook Story** | **9:16** | Stories, vertical ads |
| **Reels / TikTok** | **9:16** | Short video |
| **Facebook cover** | **820 × 312 px** (or 16:9 safe zone) | Page header |
| **Link preview / OG** | **1200 × 630 px** (1.91:1) | Website shares |

**Safe zones:** Keep logo, headline, and CTA inside the center 80% on 9:16 — avoid top/bottom UI overlap on Stories and Reels.

## 8. Social post templates

### Template A — Awareness (single image, 4:5)

- White or soft photo + 20% white overlay
- Top: cyan label — `تثقيف طبي`
- Headline: bold Arabic, 1 line
- Body: 2–3 short lines, gray text
- Bottom: cyan pill CTA — `تواصل واتساب`
- Corner: CliniCairo● (LTR)

### Template B — Carousel (3–5 slides, 1:1 or 4:5)

1. Hook question (pain point)
2. Why it matters
3. How CliniCairo helps
4. Social proof ("+6,000 حالة ناجحة")
5. CTA slide — cyan background, white text

### Template C — Reel / Story (9:16)

- Open with bold text on clean background
- Quick cuts: doctor credibility → patient journey → WhatsApp sticker
- End card: logo + +201140988255 + "ابدأ رحلة إنقاص الوزن"

### Template D — Trust / Doctor (4:5)

- Doctor photo or icon
- Title in cyan
- 3 bullets with check-circle icons
- Footer: WhatsApp + Facebook links
